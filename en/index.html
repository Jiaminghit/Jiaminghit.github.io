Method and current progress+

Joint Reconstruction with Geometrically Consistent 3D Gaussians from Heterogeneous Aerial and Ground Views

Cross-view alignment: Built a five-stage “retrieval–matching–association–alignment–evaluation” pipeline. Integrated MASt3R for retrieval and matching, associated 2D correspondences with SfM 3D points, and combined RANSAC initialization with seven-parameter Sim(3) optimization constrained by bidirectional reprojection to align aerial and ground coordinate systems. Evaluated the camera poses corresponding to 5,419 ground-view images against a reference in the Park scene of the Horizon-GS dataset, achieving a normalized positional RMSE of 0.286% of the reference scale and a rotational RMSE of 0.197°.

3D Gaussian reconstruction: Reproduced 3DGS and 2DGS on self-captured scenes and developed an understanding of the CUDA implementation of differentiable Gaussian rasterization.

012D annotation quality inspection75.0% / 84.5% Precision / Recall proxy metrics+

Integrated camera 2D detection algorithms into the LiDAR 3D dynamic annotation pipeline to pre-screen anomalies before manual quality inspection. Implemented multi-camera projection of 3D boxes, ego-frame ROI filtering, and 2D–3D instance association; used normalized overlap ratios and the Hungarian algorithm for global matching, and routed anomalies into three categories: suspected false positives, occlusion, and out-of-view cases. Designed task-level quality metrics for 3D precision and 2D recall. Matched 64,600 instance pairs across 8 production-data test CLIPs (3,200 frames), achieving Precision and Recall proxy metrics of 75.0% and 84.5% for the main dynamic classes (Car, Truck, Bicycle, Pedestrian). Automatically flagged 12,100 suspected false positives and identified 11,800 potential missed detections to focus manual review on anomalous results;

02Annotation post-processing99.86% candidate Feature processing coverage+

Encapsulated dynamic trajectory interpolation using CTRV-EKF and RTS smoothing, together with robust aggregation of static objects in world coordinates, and integrated both algorithms into the dynamic annotation pipeline. Converted 189,200 / 189,500 candidate Features in test cases, achieving 99.86% processing coverage. Developed frame-wise slicing of aggregated static annotations into ego-frame annotations, combining 6DoF coordinate transformations, five-stream point-cloud fusion, and KD-tree/OBB filtering to generate per-frame ground-truth static-object annotations, reducing the manual static-object annotation workload to 1/44 of the original level;
